Signal path

From magnetic field to a result

Each step below is what the device's chip computes, 25 times a second. The charts show the last 20 seconds. Hover a chart to read values.

1 · Raw field change at the sensor

Up-down axisHead-to-toe axis

The sensor feels both magnets on all three axes, so no axis is a clean chest or belly signal yet. Two axes are shown. Units: nanotesla (nT). Earth's field (about 45,000 nT) is removed by the filter.

2 · Separated chest and belly movement

ChestBelly

The calibration presses tell the device how each magnet shows up on the sensor's three axes. With that, it unmixes the three readings into two movements, in millimetres.

3 · Chest against belly

Plot chest against belly and breathing traces a loop. In step, it is a thin diagonal line. As the chest falls behind, the loop opens. Fully paradoxical, it flips to the other diagonal.

4 · Timing gap over time

Recomputed every second over a 20-second window. Grey marks are windows the device threw away because the baby moved or the breathing wasn't regular.

5 · Breathing rate over time

The dashed line is the WHO fast-breathing cut-off for this child's age.

6 · Signal quality

How regular the last 20 seconds of breathing were, from 0 to 1. Below 0.4, or while the baby is moving, the window is not used.

Decision

How the device chooses a colour

Green · no actionRate under the WHO cut-off for the child's age, and the timing gap under the age band's lower limit.
Amber · recheck in 24 hFast breathing, or a timing gap between the two limits.
Red · refer to PHC todayA timing gap at or above the upper limit, or fast breathing together with a raised gap.
